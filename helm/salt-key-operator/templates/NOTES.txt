objects in namespaces where a salt-master-kubernetes release's trusted-

install/upgrade that chart with:
  agent.trustedMinions.enabled=true

To trust a minion (replacing `salt-key -a <minion-id>`), apply a
SaltMinionKey in the SAME namespace as the target salt-master-kubernetes
release:

  apiVersion: salt.saltstack.io/v1alpha1
  kind: SaltMinionKey
  metadata:
    name: <minion-id>
    namespace: <namespace>
  spec:
    minionId: <minion-id>
    publicKey: |
      -----BEGIN PUBLIC KEY-----
      ...
      -----END PUBLIC KEY-----

Then watch it get accepted:
  kubectl get saltminionkey <minion-id> -n <namespace> -o wide

Propagation to already-running master pods happens via the normal kubelet
ConfigMap-volume resync (bounded by kubelet's periodic sync, on the order of
a minute) - no `salt-key -a`, no master pod restart required.
